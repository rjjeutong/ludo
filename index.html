How to play

Night Carnival house rules.

🎲 The turn

Roll the die. A 6 means roll again — keep rolling until a non-6 lands. All your rolls form a dice queue; then spend them, one move per die, in any order, on any tokens. A 6 can instead deploy a token from base (or free a prisoner).

♻ Play every die

You must dispatch so that as many dice as possible get played. A choice that strands a die is illegal if another choice plays them all — this outranks even capturing.

⚔ Captures are mandatory

Landing on a square holding opponent pieces captures them all — entry squares included. If a max-dice dispatch can reach a capture, you must take a capturing route; with several options, you choose which. A piece that captures is spent — it rests until your next turn, so your remaining dice go to other pieces.

⛓ Prisoner rule (optional)

Captured tokens go to the capturer's jail instead of back home. The owner must spend a rolled 6 to free one back to base — then deploy it later with another 6.

🧱 Wall rule (optional)

Two or more of your tokens on one square form a wall. No token may pass over it, and opponents can't land on it — but you can land more of your own pieces there to grow the wall as tall as you like. Move stacked tokens off to break it.

🏆 Winning

Race clockwise around the board, then up your colored home column. A piece finishes by landing on the last colored square by exact count — a die that would overshoot can't be used on that piece. Finished pieces leave the board. First player to bring all tokens home wins.
